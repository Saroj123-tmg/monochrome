About GreenVest

Where Finance Meets Environmental Responsibility.

GreenVest Finance Pvt. Ltd. is a sustainable finance and green investment advisory company based in Kathmandu, Nepal.

We focus on connecting financial opportunities with environmental sustainability through green investment advisory, green banking consulting, ESG analysis, carbon finance advisory and financial research.

Our approach emphasizes research, transparency and customized solutions to help clients make informed and responsible financial decisions.

🌿 Our Vision

To contribute to a financial ecosystem where economic progress and environmental sustainability work together to create long-term value.


🎯 Our Approach

We combine financial research, sustainability analysis and a global perspective to develop solutions aligned with our clients' needs.
